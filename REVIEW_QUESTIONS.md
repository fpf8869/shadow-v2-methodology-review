# Request for independent methodological critique

Shadow V2 is an experimental NCAAF score model, not approved for production or wagering.

1. Check the common-Gamma Poisson joint PMF, means, variances, covariance, nonnegative support, truncation and normalization.
2. Evaluate whether fixed dispersion k=20 is defensible only as a frozen research comparison, and propose a training-only estimator without tuning on the already exposed 58-game cohort.
3. Evaluate joint exact-score log loss, Brier scores, paired uncertainty intervals, CRPS and calibration diagnostics.
4. Investigate observed home-win calibration mismatch (about 71% predicted versus 55% realized on an exposed 58-game cohort).
5. Audit original source publication timestamps, pre-kickoff cutoffs, immutable hashes and third-party timestamp attestations.
6. Critique the prospective protocol of at least 100 eligible future games across four weeks, with predeclared acceptance criteria.

Please report concrete mathematical or code defects, reproducible counterexamples and recommendations. Comments are not automatic independent approval. Historical exposed results are diagnostic only. No MGM EV, FIRE, or production certification.
